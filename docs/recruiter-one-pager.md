# Dark Factory OS: Recruiter One-Pager

## Headline

Dark Factory OS is a governed, open-source platform for AI-native enterprise operations. It orchestrates supervisor and specialist agents, durable jobs, portable skills, memory, knowledge retrieval, approvals, observability, and cost controls across finance, retail/CPG, and SaaS workflows.

## What It Proves Technically

- Stateful orchestration with supervisor/specialist routing.
- Typed tool integration through MCP-style adapters.
- Portable `SKILL.md` procedural memory.
- Postgres/pgvector memory plus optional GraphRAG.
- Human-in-the-loop approvals and ActionReadinessPacks.
- Trace-based evals, cost monitoring, and audit logs.
- Protocol literacy across MCP, WebMCP, A2A, and UCP.

## What It Proves Commercially

The same platform can support finance operations, retail/CPG operations, and SaaS operations without rebuilding the core. That demonstrates AI transformation judgment: reusable architecture, vertical-specific workflows, governed autonomy, and measurable business outcomes.

## Three Demo Scenarios

| Scenario | Business story | Technical proof |
|---|---|---|
| Finance AP exception | Resolve invoice exceptions with policy citations and approval gates | Governance, audit, evidence bundles |
| Retail promo rebalance | Diagnose margin collapse and propose price/inventory changes | Agentic commerce, WebMCP, UCP simulator |
| SaaS incident triage | Correlate tickets, deploys, telemetry, and accounts | Long-running ops, memory, traceability |
